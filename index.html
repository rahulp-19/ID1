PROCESS

Our Paving Process

01

Site Survey

We review the topography, estimate load weights, and design details for optimal drainage slope.

02

Subgrade Prep

We clear topsoil, excavate to exact engineering depths, and compact the base soil thoroughly.

03

Aggregate Base

We install a dense, crushed-stone base course to distribute load weight and prevent future cracking.

04

Precision Paving

We lay premium hot-mix asphalt utilizing modern, laser-guided commercial paving machinery.

05

Compaction

We perform multi-pass steel-drum rolling for maximum density, followed by lane stripe marking.
